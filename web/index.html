Station identity

Type a grid square and the position fills in (centre of the square), or type latitude/longitude and the grid square fills in. For example, North Post Radio, Trinidad: call sign 9YL, grid FK90. The name and call sign are used on the console, in reports, broadcasts and alert messages; ranges and bearings are measured from this position.

AIS receivers

Internet AIS (IS)

Shows ships from the worldwide aisstream.io network when the radio is off or out of range. Free key: go to aisstream.io, sign in with GitHub, open API Keys and create one. Ships from the internet are marked IS, ships your radio hears RF. Internet data is never sent on to VesselTracker or other sharing networks.

HF receiver

Incident files

Each incident folder holds a printable report (position, track, vessels nearby that could assist, draft MAYDAY RELAY), the raw AIS data and copies of voice recordings made during the incident.

DSC receivers (VHF Ch70 and HF, receive-only)

Distress alerts and relays raise an emergency, put the casualty on the chart and open an incident. Urgency calls raise a warning; safety, routine and test calls are logged. Daily DSC logs are kept in data/logs.
